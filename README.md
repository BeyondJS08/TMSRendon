# TMSRendon
---
This a complete TMS + ERP software system for logistic transport enterprises:
- Transport Maintenance, Management, Performance Tracking
- Operators Management and Performance Tracking
- Logistic Operation Management and Planning
- Fuel Management
- Tire Tracking and Management
- Human Resources
- Accounting with CONTPAQi
- Finance
- Warehouse
- Actives Inventory
- Costumer Relationship Management
- User Management
- Reports and KPI's
- Tracking Transports Unities and GPS

Future integrations:
- Business Intelligence advanced dashboards
- Invoices and Taxes with SAT
